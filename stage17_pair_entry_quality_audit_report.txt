2) FILTROS QUE EXISTIAM ANTES DE Stage17_OpenPair()
   Stage17_CanOpenPair() (linhas originais 20602-20705) verificava SOMENTE:
      - Stage17 habilitada / MaxActivePairs >= 1
      - par ativo existente / cooldown 180s / posicao comum aberta
      - core/security/trade permission / risk locked
      - dailyDD <= 0.50%
      - spread <= 45 pontos
      - g_market.mode != MARKET_SIDEWAYS (range)
      - g_market.mode != MARKET_ERRATIC/MARKET_SPREAD_DANGER (choppy)
      - conta hedging / limite de exposicao

      - NENHUM check de ADX
      - NENHUM check de directionalStrength (g_buy/sellStrengthScore)
      - NENHUM check de momentum (g_momentumScore)
      - NENHUM check de flow (FLOW_BALANCED/FLOW_DANGEROUS passavam)
      - NENHUM check de ATR (MARKET_DEAD e MARKET_UNKNOWN passavam!)
      - NENHUM check de buyScore/sellScore/directionGap
      - NENHUM conceito de runner potential
   Os bloqueios de RANGE/CHOPPY eram reais (bloqueavam mesmo, nao eram so
   log), MAS a classificacao g_market.mode e grosseira: todo mercado
